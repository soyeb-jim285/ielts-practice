# PRODUCT.md — IELTS Practice

## Register
product — an app UI (dashboard, practice sessions, results, settings). Design serves the task; it should disappear into practice.

## Users and purpose
IELTS candidates (typically targeting band 6.5–8) practising Speaking and Writing on laptop and phone, often at night, in focused 20–60 minute sessions. They record answers or write timed essays, then study scored feedback: bands per criterion, exact mistakes with timestamps, fluency metrics, a band+1 rewrite, and a spaced-review deck.

## Desired outcome
Measurable band improvement through realistic timed practice, honest descriptor-anchored scoring, focused feedback (top 3 fixes), and repetition loops.

## Brand personality
Calm, precise, encouraging, trustworthy — a good tutor, not a game. Quiet confidence; no hype.

## Anti-references
Generic AI SaaS look (glow buttons, gradient blobs, pill badges everywhere, uniform card grids, hero-metric templates), gamified toy UIs, cream/sand "paper" defaults, dense admin-panel clutter.

## Principles
1. Reading comfort first: essays, transcripts and prompts are long-form reading surfaces.
2. Density where data lives (results, history), air where attention matters (exam screens).
3. One primary action per view; exam screens are distraction-free.
4. Honesty in feedback UI: ranges, calibration state and uncertainty are always visible.
5. WCAG AA everywhere; keyboard and reduced motion supported.
