# Speech-to-text models for speaking scoring

What we need from STT: a **verbatim** transcript (um/uh, repetitions, false starts, learner grammar kept as spoken), **word times** that line up with the audio, and ideally per-word confidence. Fluency scoring and the transcript highlight both read the word times.

**Labels.** *Measured* = our own runs (`.eval/stt-bakeoff/REPORT.md`, `.eval/stt-verbatim2`, 3 TTS clips of 41-59 s with known scripts, plus the 2026-10-04 timing test below). *Literature* = published numbers or vendor pricing, not re-run by us. *Provider* values are list prices and change.

## Replay and highlight timing (2026-10-04)

Test: 30 s mic-style recording (Chrome MediaRecorder webm/opus, fake microphone playing TTS speech with a 6.4 s silence in the middle) plus an 87 s file (clip, 5 s silence, same clip), run through both models.

- Time base: the recorded file lines up with the source audio within 17 ms. In Chrome the `<audio>` clock agrees with the content after seeking to 0, 3, 12, 17.5 and 25 s (it plays 2.50 s in 2.51 s of wall time). The webm has no duration in its header (`Infinity`), which the player already handles, but seeking works.
- Both models: the second copy of the clip comes back 0.000 s later than the first plus the 5 s gap (median over 125-127 matched words), so there is no cumulative drift and a long pause does not move later words.
- Whisper stretches words backward over pauses and dropped fillers. After a 0.84 s pause it shows "I" as 47.53-48.23 s while the voice is at 48.30 s. Scribe gives 48.30-48.32 s. This is what made the highlight lead the voice and stay on a word through a pause.
- Fix: `alignWords` in `packages/core/src/timing.ts` trims every word to its voiced part using the recorder's 50 ms energy frames (and the stretch rule for older results). On the Whisper output, silence inside words drops from 1.75 s to 1.07 s; Scribe stays at 0.17 s.

## Recommendation

Keep Scribe v2 as default and Whisper large-v3 (primed) as fallback; both are verbatim and keep word times. Do not offer gpt-4o-transcribe, Deepgram or AssemblyAI through OpenRouter: they remove the hesitations the fluency criterion is judged on. A direct AssemblyAI client with `disfluencies: true` is the one candidate worth a trial (literature filler F1 about 92) and would also add per-word confidence; it needs its own key and was not run here.
