NextBand · symbol v3

Rebuilt on a grid taken from the wordmark. Left stem stops at the x-height (lowercase), right stem rises to cap height (capital): the letter climbs one level, the way a score climbs one band. Straight edges, softened corners, stem = the wordmark's own stem.

01 · Construction

Unit s = 10, the wordmark n stem. Width 4s = stem + counter 2s + stem. Left stem stops at x-height, the flat top sits on cap height, so the type sets the roof angle: 30.0°. Roof 0.9s thick, square to the slope. Fillets r = 2s, s, s/4 (circles shown for the two large ones). Height ≈ 6s = 1.5 × width (2:3), a capital-N proportion. Dotted = sharp skeleton, fill = final.
Symbol is the capital N: same baseline, cap height, and n→e spacing (7.1). Blue box = clear space, 2s (one counter width) on every side.
